To win a hackathon, your demo video needs to look less like a casual screen recording and more like a polished, high-energy product launch (think Apple or Stripe keynotes). Acting as your own professional video editor and motion designer, you should focus on clarity, pacing, and visual engagement.

The Visual Aesthetic & Motion GraphicsJudges review dozens of videos; clean, intentional motion graphics will instantly make yours stand out.

The "Pop & Zoom" Framing: Never just show a static, full-screen browser window. Crop in tightly on the feature you are talking about. Use smooth scale transitions (e.g., zooming in 110% over 15 frames) to guide the judge's eyes to the exact button or data point mattering most.

Sleek Screen Frames: Place your screen recordings inside a clean, minimalist 3D or 2D device mockup (like a bezel-less iPhone or a subtle MacBook frame) floating over a dark, neutral, or slightly gradient background.

Kinetic Typography & Lower Thirds: When you introduce yourself or a massive stat (e.g., "Reduces latency by 40%"), don't just say it. Use bold, clean kinetic typography that scales up quickly and fades out. Use professional fonts.

Accent Pointers: Replace your default mouse cursor in post-production with a custom, high-visibility cursor. Add a subtle radial pulse effect whenever you click an element to make the software feel responsive.
